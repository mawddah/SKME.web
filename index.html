Who We Are

Leading water treatment provider in Saudi Arabia since 2006.

Suido Kiko Middle East (SKME) was established in 2006 as a joint venture between Suido Kiko Kaisha of Japan—a subsidiary of TORAY Industries Inc.—and Saudi Brothers Commercial Company Group.

Headquartered in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, SKME specializes in turnkey Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) services for municipal and industrial water and wastewater treatment projects across the Kingdom.

Our Goals & Commitment

Why industrial & municipal clients rely on SKME.

Committed to client-oriented solutions that suit your operational demands. We combine strict quality standards with sustainable practices to deliver lasting water infrastructure.

Discuss Your Project Goals →

We commit to delivering water and wastewater projects on schedule—ensuring minimal disruption, disciplined stage planning, and maximum operational efficiency for our clients across Saudi Arabia.

We guarantee top quality in every plant we engineer and construct—employing proven treatment processes (MBR, SBR, MBBR, ASP, SWRO, BWRO) and adhering to stringent quality benchmarks for long-term reliability.

Leveraging advanced Japanese water technology and over 70 years of engineering expertise from our parent company Suido Kiko Kaisha (TORAY Group), we deliver cutting-edge solutions tailored to the environmental challenges of the Middle East.

Our water reuse and recycling systems help industries and municipalities drastically reduce water wastage, recover treated effluent, and support sustainable water management across KSA.
